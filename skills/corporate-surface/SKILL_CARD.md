# Skill Card: corporate-surface

## Description
Improves partner-facing and institutional pages by promoting real proof, reducing generic SaaS patterns, and tightening hierarchy around clear business outcomes.

## Owner
ABVX / Anton Biletskiy-Volokh

## License
MIT. See repository LICENSE.

## Intended Use
Use for corporate landings, ecosystem/capability pages, partner-facing sections, and other public business surfaces that must feel credible and product-backed.

## Out of Scope
Do not use for editorial longform pages, utilitarian dashboards, or speculative branding exercises disconnected from real products or outputs.

## Sources and Attribution
ABVX adapted from bergside `awesome-design-skills` corporate pattern library, narrowed around proof-first partner-facing structure and anti-template corrections: https://github.com/bergside/awesome-design-skills

## Inputs and Outputs
Inputs: brief, screenshots, approved copy, real product assets, links, and existing design constraints.

Outputs: business-job framing, proof-surface prioritization, hierarchy corrections, and desktop/mobile credibility checks.

## Risks and Mitigations
- Risk: page still reads like generic SaaS. Mitigation: prioritize real product proof over equal-card feature grids.
- Risk: architecture jargon outruns business clarity. Mitigation: state the partner job first and translate capability into outcomes.
- Risk: decorative polish displaces CTA clarity. Mitigation: preserve explicit contact and partner-entry paths.
